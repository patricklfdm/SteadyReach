# Choose the deliverable with the engineer

[简体中文](SITE_DELIVERY_CN.md) · [Review and recovery](ENGINEERING_DECISIONS_EN.md)

Select the site type on the home page. Open **Materials and delivery conditions**, choose **Use these delivery conditions**, and choose the intended use under **Intended use and flow paths**. A new site or editable revision can adopt the template. Reopen the review page to check the frozen conditions. A custom agreement is also available. Keep the client's thresholds and their basis with the project.

| Purpose | River between bridges | One culvert crossing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Conditional screening | Place the limits just downstream of the upper bridge and just upstream of the lower bridge. Confirm reach, flow direction, discharge, active end conditions, terrain datum, bed representation and roughness. State possible bridge backwater. | Identify each barrel group, opening dimensions, length and inverts, blockage, road crest, tailwater, discharge and roughness. Show assumptions beside headwater and tailwater. |
| Compare alternatives | Carry forward the common reach, event and datum. Match baseline and revision sections. Trace changes in water level, wet ends and overbank flow. | Keep event, datum and reference crossing comparable. Vary a supported opening, obstruction or control and inspect headwater, roadway and barrel flows. |
| Submit for engineering review | Supply source-linked submerged sections, an independent interior stage with a registered tolerance, and a single-factor boundary study for each selected profile. Review the extent of the boundary influence. | Supply traced group dimensions, length, both inverts on the model datum, and surveyed road profiles. Verify the native roadway flow for each selected profile. Enter a contract road margin and basis when applicable. Inspect entrance, alternate paths and blockage. |

All three uses check dry section ends. A wet endpoint requires checking connectivity and geometry. A comparison additionally requires comparable computed editions. Formal review requires confirmation that the site is within the supported single flow path. Wet ends, errors outside a registered tolerance and unverified required records stay visible. Some checks require the engineer to inspect the original drawings or physical setting.

Changing only the purpose on an editable edition can reuse terrain, recorded geometry and hydrologic sources. Review any new evidence conditions before starting a new calculation. The original edition keeps its own scope. A result can be shared for diagnostic review when its intended-use conditions are still open.

Open the result priorities for the specific measurement, affected station or revision comparison. Consistency and missing native evidence take precedence over hydraulic interpretation. Reports and the offline recipient guide carry the selected scope and the primary engineering action. The **Minimal** package contains the portable native model. The **Complete** package adds the technical reports, evidence and available GIS deliverables. Check the package manifest before promising a mapped delivery. Building-specific inundation, bridge performance and final design require their own evidence and engineering review.
